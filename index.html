The Tale
of Two Strangers

Read slowly

Once upon a time, in a land not so different from ours, there lived a young girl and a young boy.

They were strangers.

They had never met.

They had never heard each other's voices.

They knew nothing about one another.

Yet, somehow, life had already decided that their paths would cross.

The girl was beautiful, but what made her special wasn't only her beauty.

She had a heart that could love deeply, even though she loved to distance herself from people.

The boy was different.

He wasn't the type who easily explained what was happening inside him.

He could be quiet when he had a thousand things on his mind.

He could seem unbothered when, deep down, he cared more than he wanted anyone to know.

He had learned to carry his problems quietly.

Then, one ordinary day, something unexpected happened.

Two strangers found each other at a not really random spwan point, Random hellos and checkups turned into routines .

Neither expected it to be this big.

But it became big.

At first, they were simply two people talking.

Then the conversations became something they looked forward to.

Slowly, the stranger became familiar.

The familiar became important.

And the important became someone neither wanted to lose.

But love is rarely a straight road.

There were moments when the girl wondered about the boy.

“Why does he act like he doesn't care?”

“Why can he be so distant sometimes?”

“Why doesn't he just tell me what he's feeling?”

And perhaps those questions were fair.

Because the boy sometimes forgot that silence can be misunderstood.

He sometimes forgot that when you love someone, they cannot always see the battles you're fighting inside your head.

There were things he was dealing with.

Things he couldn't always explain.

Things his personality made him keep hidden.

He wasn't trying to punish her with silence.

He wasn't trying to make her feel unwanted.

He simply didn't know how to open every locked door inside himself.

And yet, despite all of that...

He loved her.

Quietly (sometimes loudly ... maybe too loud)

Deeply.

In ways he didn't always know how to put into words.

He loved the way she could make ordinary conversations feel important.

He loved the way she could frustrate him and still be the person he wanted to talk to.

He loved the fact that, somehow, she had become part of his everyday thoughts.

And perhaps she didn't always realize it, but she had become one of the few people capable of reaching a heart that usually kept everyone at a distance.

Their story wasn't perfect.

They misunderstood each other.

They annoyed each other.

Sometimes they probably wondered whether they were doing this love thing correctly.

But every time they could have walked away...

They stayed.

And maybe that was the most beautiful part of their story.

Because love isn't always about finding someone who never hurts you.

Sometimes it's finding someone worth understanding.

Someone worth being patient with.

Someone worth growing with.

Someone you choose even after you've seen their imperfections.

And somewhere along the journey, the boy realized something.

He wasn't talking to a stranger anymore.

He was talking to someone who had slowly become a part of him.

Someone whose happiness mattered.

Someone whose absence would hurt.

Someone he wanted beside him, even on the days when he didn't know how to say it.

And the girl eventually became more than just someone he met.

She became the person hidden inside his quiet thoughts.

The person behind some of his smiles.

The person he worried about without always admitting it.

The person he could never honestly call just another person.

And now, dear reader...

Perhaps you've started wondering who these two people are.

Maybe you've been trying to figure out which tale this is.

Maybe you thought this was simply another story about two strangers who happened to fall in love.

But it isn't.

The girl in this story...

is you.

And the boy...

is me.

The girl is Peace (she doesn't like the name 😂).

And the boy is Godswill.

Two people who somehow found each other in a class when neither probably expected to find something that would become this meaningful.

And if you ever wondered why I sometimes seem distant...

I want you to know something.

It isn't because I don't love you.

Sometimes its just on impulse...

Sometimes my stoic nature makes me keep things inside when I should probably let you in.

Sometimes I may not say "I love you" in all the ways you need to hear it.

But that doesn't mean the feeling isn't there.

It is.

And if I could change one thing, it would be learning how to make you feel the love that I sometimes struggle to express.

Because the truth is...

I didn't expect to find you.

I didn't know a random routine conversation daily would lead me to someone whose presence would eventually mean this much to me.

I didn't know that a stranger would become someone I would care about this deeply.

And I definitely didn't know that one day, I'd be writing a love tale just to reveal that the two characters were us all along.

So this isn't really a story about a boy and a girl.

It's about you and me.

About where we started.

About where we are.

And hopefully, about all the chapters we haven't written yet.

Because I don't want our story to end with:

“And they lived happily ever after.”

I want it to continue.

Through the beautiful days.

Through the difficult ones.

Through the misunderstandings.

Through the laughter.

Through the growth.

Through everything life decides to put in front of us.

I want more chapters.

More memories.

More reasons to look back one day and say,

“Look how far we came.”

So, baby...

If you ever question how much you mean to me, remember this little tale.

Remember that somewhere between two strangers, random conversations, misunderstandings, silence, laughter and everything in between...

I found you.

And I wouldn't trade that part of my story for anything.

Happy Girlfriend's Day, my love.

Cheri
